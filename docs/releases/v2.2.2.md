## AnixOps SSH 2.2.2

### 改进 / Improvements

- **Pageant** On Windows the built-in engine also asks Pageant (PuTTY's
  agent) for keys, after the OpenSSH agent.
- **手机端多级跳板机** On mobile, a jump host that itself goes through a jump
  host now works: the chain is resolved hop by hop (up to 8 hops), and a
  loop is reported instead of hanging.
