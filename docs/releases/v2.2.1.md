## AnixOps SSH 2.2.1

### 改进 / Improvements

- **传输进度** Uploads and folder downloads now show a percentage, bytes
  moved out of the total, speed and time left once a queued transfer
  starts. Folders count the sum of their files.
- **错误带路径** A failed transfer names the remote path and the reason,
  for example `opening /var/log/app.log: permission denied`.
